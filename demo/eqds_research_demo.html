Company Wiki

Every wiki is built by GIC's research agents, who debate each page from their sources, and refreshes itself. Each morning at 07:30 SGT a wiki is refreshed when, since it was built, the company has reported earnings (two days after, once the call and the results notes are in), 6 or more new broker reports or 3 or more rating or target changes have arrived, 3 reports have landed within two days, or 30 days have passed with anything new. A refresh rebuilds only the pages the news makes stale. Broker flow waits 14 days after the last build and the week before a scheduled release, and a wiki is rebuilt in full 120 days after its last full build.
